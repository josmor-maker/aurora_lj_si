## Short version

- Commuting by train or bus: Aurora.
- Driving in: Pisarna (54 free parking spaces) or MP hub (free parking).
- Centre, big network: Impact Hub.
- Small and cheap, by Tivoli: makerSP_CE (18 desks).
- Private office for a team: Pisarna or MP hub. Aurora has no private offices.
