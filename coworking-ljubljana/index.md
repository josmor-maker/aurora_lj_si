# Coworking in Ljubljana · Aurora Coworking

Aurora is a member-run coworking space at Železna cesta 14, level 1, 1000 Ljubljana, a short walk from the main train and bus station.

- 24/7 access for members
- Two call booths (Zürich, Wrocław) and two meeting rooms (Bohinj, up to 10 people; Otočec)
- Free coffee, kitchen with fridge

## Prices (per month, + VAT)

| Desk | Any free desk | Your own desk |
