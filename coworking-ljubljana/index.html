Ljubljana coworking guide

Coworking spaces in Ljubljana, compared

Five spaces with published prices, side by side: what a desk costs, day passes, 24/7 access, meeting rooms and where each one is. Prices checked on 26 November 2026.

Written by Aurora Coworking Ljubljana, so yes, we're one of the five. Every price below comes from each space's own website (linked), and we say plainly where another space is a better fit.

The short version

  • Commuting by train or bus: Aurora, a short walk from the station.
  • Driving in: Pisarna (54 free parking spaces) or MP hub (free parking).
  • Right in the centre, big network: Impact Hub on Slovenska cesta.
  • Small and cheap, by Tivoli park: makerSP_CE, 18 desks.
  • Need a private office for a team: Pisarna or MP hub publish office prices; Aurora doesn't offer private offices.

Each space in a few lines

Aurora Coworking Ljubljana

Run by its members, not a property company. Big windows, two soundproof call booths (Zürich and Wrocław), two meeting rooms (Bohinj seats 10), a lounge with sofas and a high table, an espresso machine and a kitchen. 24/7 access. No private offices. See the 3D map or book a free first visit.

Pisarna

In the Elma business building in Črnuče, north of the centre. Hot desks booked through an app, fixed desks, private offices for 2–4 people and three meeting rooms. The big plus is 54 free parking spaces; there's also a run club and a padel league.

MP hub

Run by Mladi podjetnik in Šiška, next to the Aleja shopping centre. About 100 workstations including private offices (from 460€ + VAT for two people), free parking, and a virtual office / company address from 25€ + VAT. Meeting rooms can also be rented by non-members.

Impact Hub Ljubljana

Part of the global Impact Hub network, on Slovenska cesta in the centre. Part-Time and Full-Time memberships, lower prices for NGOs and social enterprises, lots of community events. Member hours are weekdays 8:00–20:00 rather than 24/7.

makerSP_CE

A small space (18 desks) on the Kampus site next to Tivoli park. Monthly, weekly and daily passes, a call room, free printing and free parking. It's popular, so call ahead to check there's a desk.

How to choose

  • Location beats price. A 20€ difference a month disappears if you spend an extra 30 minutes a day getting there.
  • Count your calls. If you're on video calls every day, look for booths or a call room: Aurora has two booths, makerSP_CE a call room.
  • Check the hours. If you work early, late or at weekends, pick a 24/7 space.
  • Visit first. Every space feels different with people in it. Most offer a day pass or a trial; Aurora's first visit is free.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest coworking in Ljubljana?

Of the spaces with published prices, Pisarna's hot desk (110€ + VAT a month) and makerSP_CE (135€ a month including VAT, about 111€ without) are the lowest. Aurora's Flex is 120€ + VAT. Prices checked 26 November 2026.

Which coworking spaces in Ljubljana are open 24/7?

Aurora, Pisarna, MP hub and makerSP_CE all list 24/7 access for members. Impact Hub lists member hours of Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00.

Can I get a day pass?

makerSP_CE sells a day for 20€ (VAT included), Impact Hub for 29€ (VAT included) and MP hub for 30€ + VAT. At Aurora your first visit is free, coffee included.

Which coworking is closest to Ljubljana train station?

Aurora, at Železna cesta 14, is a short walk from the main train and bus station. Impact Hub is on Slovenska cesta in the city centre.
