Coworking Ljubljana

Coworking in Ljubljana, by the train station

Aurora is a member-run coworking space at Železna cesta 14. Big windows, 24/7 access, two call booths, two meeting rooms, a kitchen and good coffee. From 120€ a month.

Why people pick Aurora

Most coworking spaces in Ljubljana are run as real-estate businesses. Aurora isn't. It's a self-sustaining community run for and by its members, part of Slovenia's Aurora Coworking network. That keeps prices simple and the room friendly: founders, developers, designers and freelancers who actually know each other.

  • Location: Železna cesta 14, level 1, a short walk from Ljubljana's main train and bus station. Easy if you commute from Kranj, Maribor, Celje or Novo mesto.
  • Hours: 24/7 access for members, weekends included.
  • Calls: two soundproof call booths, Zürich and Wrocław.
  • Meetings: two meeting rooms, Bohinj (up to 10 people) and Otočec.
  • Kitchen: espresso machine and grinder, fridge, dishwasher.
  • Lounge: a glass-walled corner room with sofas and a high table for lunch.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How much is coworking in Ljubljana?

At Aurora, Flex is 120€ + VAT a month and a fixed desk is 180€ + VAT a month. Both include 24/7 access, call booths, meeting rooms and coffee.

Where is the coworking space?

Železna cesta 14, level 1, 1000 Ljubljana: a short walk from the main train and bus station.

Can I try the space first?

Yes. Book a first visit and work from a desk for the day. The coffee is on us.

Is Aurora open 24/7?

Yes, members have access around the clock, weekends included.
