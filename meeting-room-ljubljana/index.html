Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms and call booths in Ljubljana

Two glass-walled meeting rooms and two soundproof call booths at Železna cesta 14, included in every Aurora membership.

How booking works

Each room and booth has its own calendar. Members book a slot and show up. No credits, no hourly fees: rooms are part of the membership, Flex (120€) or Fixed (180€).

See where they are on the 3D map of the space.

Getting there for a meeting

Železna cesta 14, level 1, is a short walk from Ljubljana's main train and bus station. Guests coming in from another city can be in the room minutes after they arrive.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Are the meeting rooms included in the membership?

Yes. Flex and Fixed members book the Bohinj and Otočec rooms and the two call booths on a shared calendar, at no extra cost.

How many people fit in the meeting room?

Bohinj seats up to 10 around one long table. Otočec is smaller, for 1:1s and small team sessions.

I'm not a member. Can I rent a room?

Write to info@auroracoworking.com with the date and the number of people and we'll see what we can do.
