Questions

Good to know

Can I see the space before joining?

Yes, and we'd like you to. Book a first visit: a member shows you around, you can work from a desk for the day, and the coffee is on us.

How much does it cost?

Flex is 120€ + VAT a month, a fixed desk is 180€ + VAT a month. Both include 24/7 access, the call booths, the meeting rooms, coffee and the kitchen.

What's the difference between Flex and Fixed?

With Flex you sit at any free desk when you come in. With Fixed the desk is yours: leave your monitors, keyboard and plants set up. Everything else is the same.

Is it really open at night and on weekends?

Yes. Members have access 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How do I book a booth or a meeting room?

Members book the Zürich and Wrocław booths and the Bohinj and Otočec meeting rooms on a shared calendar. It's included in the membership.

Who runs Aurora?

Aurora Coworking Ljubljana is a self-sustaining, community-building initiative, not a real-estate business. We focus on members, not square metres. It's part of the Aurora Coworking network, also in Murska Sobota.
