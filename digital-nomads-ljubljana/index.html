Working remotely from Ljubljana?

Step off the train, walk to Železna cesta 14, and you have a desk, a call booth for your stand-up and a room full of people who know the city.

Why Aurora works for remote workers

  • Right by the station. Arriving by train or bus from Vienna, Zagreb, Trieste or the airport shuttle, you're a short walk away.
  • Calls at any hour. 24/7 access and two soundproof booths mean US or Asia time zones aren't a problem.
  • People. An English-speaking community of founders and freelancers, plus events on Meetup.
  • Simple pricing. Flex is 120€ + VAT a month, everything included.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Ljubljana good for digital nomads?

It's small, safe, green and walkable, with good train links to Austria, Italy and Croatia. A coworking desk gives you a routine and people to meet.

Is there a minimum stay?

Memberships are monthly. Write to info@auroracoworking.com if you're in town for a shorter time.

Do I need to speak Slovenian?

No. Members speak English every day, and many come from abroad.
