Hot desk Ljubljana

A flex desk in Ljubljana for 120€ a month

Come in when you want, sit at any free desk, use the booths and meeting rooms. Flex membership at Aurora Coworking, Železna cesta 14.

How Flex works

Flex is for people whose week changes: two days in the office, a client day, a trip. You don't get a fixed desk, you get the whole space. Walk in at any hour, take a free desk, plug in.

The 3D map shows which desks are free for flex members (yellow) and which belong to fixed members (navy).

  • The Zürich and Wrocław call booths for calls

  • Community events and a room full of people to ask things

Flex or fixed?

If you come in most days and work with an external monitor, a fixed desk (180€) saves you setting up every morning. If you're in two or three days a week, Flex is the better deal.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is a hot desk?

A desk you don't own: you sit at any free desk when you come in. At Aurora that's the Flex membership.

How much is a hot desk in Ljubljana at Aurora?

120€ + VAT a month, with 24/7 access, call booths, meeting rooms and coffee included.

Can I switch from Flex to a fixed desk later?

Yes. If you start coming in every day and want your own desk, switch to Fixed (180€ + VAT a month) when one is free.
